Privacy Policy

Shrink-X Pro: Image & PDF Tools

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shrink-X Pro (“the app”) handles information when you use its image compressor, Make PDF, PDF compressor, photo enhancer, image resizer, image cropper, document scanner, and photo collage maker features.

1. Files and permissions

The app may ask for access to photos or files that you choose to process, and camera access when you use the document-scanning feature. These permissions are used to provide the feature you select. You can manage app permissions in your Android device settings. Only select files you intend to use with the app.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads for advertising. Advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic information, subject to their configuration and applicable consent choices. These services may use information to deliver, measure, limit, and prevent fraud in advertising.

Please review Unity’s privacy information for details about its processing: Unity Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

Information accessed by the app is used to provide the tools you choose, maintain app functionality, and support advertising. The precise information handled can depend on the Android permissions you grant, the feature you use, and the advertising and analytics configuration active in the installed version.

4. Storage and sharing

Files you select or create may be stored on your device according to the app’s functionality and the locations you choose. Advertising-related information may be processed by Unity and its service providers. This policy does not promise that every type of file or data is processed exclusively on-device; please avoid using the app for information you do not want processed by the app or its enabled third-party services.

5. Children’s privacy

Shrink-X Pro is not specifically designed for children. If the app is configured or distributed for children or mixed audiences, its audience settings, data practices, and advertising configuration must comply with applicable child-privacy requirements.

6. Security and retention

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect information handled by the app. Retention depends on whether information is stored by the app on your device or processed by a third-party service under its own policies. You can remove locally stored files through your device or the app’s available controls.

7. Your choices

You can deny or revoke camera and file/photo permissions in Android settings, although some features may then be unavailable. Advertising choices and device-level ad controls may be available in your Android settings, depending on your device and region.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details shown on the Shrink-X Pro Google Play listing.
